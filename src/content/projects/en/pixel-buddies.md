## How it plays

Swap tiles to reassemble a pixel illustration. Daily puzzles use a fixed seed and a challenge code lets friends play the same board on their own devices.

The workshop converts a photo into a custom puzzle on the device. It keeps the pixel artwork locally without uploading the original photo.
