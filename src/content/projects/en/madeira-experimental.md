This repository is separate from my contributions to the upstream Madeira project (those are on the [open-source page](/open-source/)). It collects experimental source and reproduction material for running a Windows x64 Vulkan route on iPad through MoltenVK.

## Limits
Compatibility is incomplete. Observations are limited to a few scenes in one game, and nothing here claims a stable full playthrough. It is not an official port.
