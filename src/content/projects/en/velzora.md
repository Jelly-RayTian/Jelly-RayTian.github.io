## Trial booking

Families can send a trial lesson request with the student's grade, subjects and availability. Staff review submissions in an admin dashboard and coordinate the next steps.

## Learning portal

The platform includes separate views for families, teachers and staff, with tools for coordinating lessons and sharing progress updates.
